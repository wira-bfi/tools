  1. A process overwrites a field that a previous process already read.
  2. The planner finds the prior writer of that field, then the earliest reader after it.
  3. From that reader it walks forward: any process reading a field in the cumulative written-set is impacted.
  4. Impacted processes get their writes reverted (except the updated fields) and are re-queued.
  5. They re-run with the new input — visible here as a second execution with a different read hash.
